## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences:

- **Staff (8+ roles):** Member, Cashier, Bookkeeper, Manager, Treasurer, BOD (Board of Directors/Secretary), and Non-member/KOICA handling, each with its own portal and route namespace. These are TTMPC cooperative office staff. Their device/desk setup is not yet fixed (could be desktop, tablet, or a counter/kiosk terminal) — don't assume a specific screen size or input method for staff portals.
- **Members:** cooperative members (teachers) using a self-service portal for loan applications, savings, payments, and profile management. Standard digital literacy — comparable to typical banking/messaging apps, no special low-literacy accommodations required.

## Product Purpose

REGANT is a web-based Decision Support System (DSS) that centralizes TTMPC's cooperative operations (membership, loans, savings, disbursement, payments) and layers ML-driven analytics on top (credit risk scoring, loan demand forecasting, MIGS scoring, debt-capacity/repayment-stress checks). Its purpose is to replace fragmented, manual cooperative processes with a centralized, predictive platform so staff and leadership can make proactive, data-driven financial decisions.

## Positioning

The mechanism a generic accounting/coop-management tool couldn't truthfully copy: parallel risk prediction (logistic regression default probability), time-series loan demand forecasting (SARIMAX), and rule-based MIGS/debt-capacity scoring — all wired directly into the operational loan workflow itself, not a bolted-on reporting dashboard.

## Operating Context

Role-based portals carry the full loan lifecycle in sequence: Member applies → Bookkeeper evaluates and performs MIGS scoring → Manager reviews/approves → Treasurer disburses → Cashier records payment. POS/grocery transactions link to member financial records. Loan forms are generated by stamping data onto existing cooperative PDF templates. Email notifications fire on loan-status changes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Status:** thesis prototype/demo — not yet handling real cooperative funds or member data in production. Actively in development (~50% complete per README). Should still read and behave as production-credible, not demo-grade.
- **Staff device context is undecided** — desktop, tablet, or kiosk are all possible; don't design staff portals around one fixed viewport.
- **Responsiveness gap:** "fully responsive UI across all role portals" is a stated roadmap item, meaning current coverage is incomplete — treat this as known debt, not a target already met.
- **Loan types:** Consolidated, Bonus, Emergency (member-only), and KOICA (non-member).
- **Terminology:** MIGS scoring (7 criteria, 0–100, threshold 50) with a 5× vs 3× loan multiplier; "bona fide" member status gates loan/savings eligibility; CBU (Capital Build-Up); PDS (Personal Data Sheet).

## Product Principles

1. Every screen belongs to one step of the loan/savings lifecycle — surface only what that role needs to act on next, not the whole system's state.
2. Analytics (risk score, MIGS, forecast) inform staff decisions; they never bypass the human approval chain.
3. Treat financial figures and role-gated actions as production-serious even pre-launch — thesis polish should never read as "just a demo."
4. Don't lock designs to one staff screen size; the device context is still open.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility standard has been required yet. Member audience has standard digital literacy, so no special low-literacy accommodations are needed. Staff device context is unconfirmed — avoid assuming a single fixed screen size for staff portals.
