For: Ashley, Gero, Romelyn, Erden, Ervine

=====================================================================
HOW THIS WORKS
=====================================================================

- One person types at a time. The others watch and say what they see.
- Each person uses their OWN private browser window (Incognito).
  One window per role, so the logins do not mix.
- Every step has three parts:
      WHO     = whose turn it is
      DO      = what to click or type
      EXPECT  = what you should see on the screen
- If what you see is different from EXPECT, STOP. Do not fix it.
  Write down: the step number, what you saw, and any red error text.
  Send it to Claude afterwards.

BRING BEFORE YOU START
  1. A payslip picture or PDF (5 MB or smaller)
  2. Ashley's real email (it must be new, never used in the system)
  3. The dashboard numbers, written down BEFORE you start:
        - number of members
        - total share capital
        - vault balance
     (we compare them again at the end)

ROLES
  Ashley   = Manager (and the new applicant in Scene 1)
  Gero     = Bookkeeper
  Romelyn  = Cashier
  Erden    = BOD and Secretary
  Ervine   = Treasurer

SCENE 1 - ASHLEY JOINS AS A NEW MEMBER (about 8 minutes)
=====================================================================

What this shows: an applicant becomes a member only after training,
payment, and BOD confirmation.

STEP 1
  WHO:    Ashley
  GO TO:  the page /membership_form (no login needed)
  DO:     Fill in the form (see the FORM VALUES list below),
          tick the declaration box, press Submit.
  EXPECT: "Application submitted successfully". Status: Pending.

STEP 2
  WHO:    Erden (logged in as BOD)
  GO TO:  /member-approvals, then open Ashley's application
  DO:     Press "Proceed to Training". Keep the email box ticked.
  EXPECT: Status changes to Training. An email arrives at Ashley's
          address.

STEP 3
  WHO:    Erden (logged in as Secretary)
  GO TO:  /Secretary_Attendance
  DO:     Set Ashley to Present, then confirm.
  EXPECT: "Saved". The row becomes locked.

STEP 4
  WHO:    Romelyn (logged in as Cashier)
  GO TO:  /Cashier_MembershipPayments
  DO:     Record two payments for Ashley:
            first  - Membership Fee: 200
            second - Initial Paid-Up Capital: 20,000
  EXPECT: Two "paid" badges and two receipts.

STEP 5
  WHO:    Erden (logged in as BOD)
  GO TO:  /member-approvals, then Ashley's application
  DO:     Press "Confirm and Complete". Keep the email box ticked.
  EXPECT: - a new membership number
          - status "Official Member"
          - exactly ONE capital row of 20,000
          - a login email is sent
  IF YOU SEE "Cannot approve ... unpaid": Step 4 is not finished.
  Go back and finish it.

STEP 6
  WHO:    Ashley
  GO TO:  the member login page
  DO:     Log in with her email and the temporary password.
          The temporary password is her SURNAME with no spaces,
          then 1234. Example: Tabiolo1234.
          The system asks for a new password. Set one.
  EXPECT: The member dashboard opens.
          Members count is up by 1.
          Total share capital is up by 20,000.

IMPORTANT - CHECK THE EMAIL AT STEP 2.
  Our email subscription may have ended.
  If no email arrives, the status still changes. Carry on.
  Give Ashley her login by hand (her email + the password rule in
  Step 6) and tell the developers (item 25 on their list).

SCENE 2 - ONE MEMBER BECOMES MIGS, ANOTHER STOPS (about 6 minutes)
=====================================================================

What this shows: MIGS points come from the cooperative's own records
and change when the records change. The pass line is 50 points.

STARTING POINT (after Recompute):
  TTMPC-416 = 46 points  (Non-MIGS)
  TTMPC-439 = 57 points  (MIGS)
  Do NOT use TTMPC-461 yet.

STEP 1
  WHO:    Gero (Bookkeeper)
  GO TO:  /migs, choose year 2026
  DO:     Press "Recompute all".
  EXPECT: 416 = 46 and 439 = 57.

STEP 2
  WHO:    Erden (Secretary)
  GO TO:  General Assembly page
  DO:     Mark TTMPC-416 as Present, then save.
  EXPECT: 416 gets +5 attendance points.

STEP 3
  WHO:    Gero (Bookkeeper)
  GO TO:  /migs-evaluate, open TTMPC-439
  DO:     Choose "Has outside loan", then confirm.
  EXPECT: Outside-loan points for 439 go from 10 down to 0.

STEP 4
  WHO:    Gero (Bookkeeper)
  GO TO:  /migs
  DO:     Press "Recompute all" again.
  EXPECT: 416 = 51 (now MIGS).
          439 = 47 (now Non-MIGS).

WHAT TO SAY TO THE PANEL:
  "Points are computed from the cooperative's own records. A declared
   outside loan or a missed meeting means missing points. A MIGS
   member can borrow up to 5 times their capital. A Non-MIGS member
   can borrow up to 3 times."

SCENE 3 - PROFILE CHANGE WITH A PAYSLIP (about 5 minutes)
=====================================================================

What this shows: a member cannot change pay or address directly.
Every change goes to the Bookkeeper with proof.

STEP 1
  WHO:    Ashley (member login)
  GO TO:  My Profile
  DO:     Change the Permanent Address AND the Net Take-Home Pay.
          Attach the payslip. Choose the pay month. Press Save.
  EXPECT: Two requests shown as "pending".
          The profile itself has NOT changed yet.

STEP 2
  WHO:    Gero (Bookkeeper)
  GO TO:  /bookkeeper-profile-changes
  DO:     Open Ashley's requests.
  EXPECT: You see the old value, the new value, and a link to the
          payslip.

STEP 3
  WHO:    Gero (Bookkeeper)
  GO TO:  same page
  DO:     APPROVE the address change.
          REJECT the net pay change and type a reason.
  EXPECT: The address is updated. The net pay is unchanged.

STEP 4
  WHO:    Ashley
  GO TO:  My Profile, open her requests
  EXPECT: One "Approved", one "Rejected" with the reason shown.

WHAT TO SAY TO THE PANEL:
  "A member cannot edit pay directly. Every change goes to the
   Bookkeeper with proof, and every decision is in the audit log."

STEP 1
  WHO:    Gero (Bookkeeper)
  GO TO:  /bookkeeper-isc
  DO:     Set the period: December 2025 to September 2026.
          Set the pool: 1,000,000. Press "Calculate".
  EXPECT: 347 members, 10 months, rate about 2.77 percent.

STEP 2
  WHO:    Gero
  DO:     Press "Post".
  EXPECT: A new posting appears in /bookkeeper-isc-journal.

STEP 3
  WHO:    Gero
  DO:     Open "payout preferences". Untick one or two members
          (they will get cash instead of more capital).
  EXPECT: Those members are marked for cash.

!!! DO NOT PRESS "SETTLE" IN THE REHEARSAL !!!
    Settle adds capital for ALL members and cannot be undone.
    Press Settle only on the real defense day.
