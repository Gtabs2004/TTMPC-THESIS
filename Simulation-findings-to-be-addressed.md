## 6. Risk band card: what the reviewer sees now and what it should say

**Seen in the screenshot (Low Risk, scored 19/10/2026, model V3.3, cached).** The band and "Standard review" are clear, but: "about 8 out of 100 loans scored like this one had repayment problems" is a training statistic that reads as a warning on a Low band and tells the reviewer nothing to do; "Points to check: nothing stands out" gives no guidance; the footer is a long italic disclaimer; "SCORED, MODEL V3.3, CACHED" is model housekeeping in the main view; "Re-run" does not say why. The text comes from `BAND_META` in `risk_model.py` (the action, the share and the bad rate), the layout from `LoanApprovalDetails.jsx`.

**Action per band (approved by the group on 2026-10-23; Gero puts it in `BAND_META`, Ervine lays it out).** Always shown under "What to do", as a short checklist, with the line "This guides the review; it is not grounds for denial."
| Band | Headline | Checklist for the reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Low Risk | Proceed with the standard review | Payslip matches the declared net pay; the monthly due is within 40 percent of net take-home pay (Debt Capacity Framework); co-maker details are complete |
| Medium Risk | Verify before approving | Compare the latest payslip with the declared net pay; ask about outside loans and salary deductions; check the last 12 months of payments for late installments; if the due is near the 40 percent limit, consider a smaller amount or a longer term; record the check in the internal remarks |
| High Risk | Review closely and record the reason | Get the payslip and the list of deductions; contact the co-maker and confirm willingness and capacity; check every earlier loan (behind, penalties, restructured); consider a lower amount or a stronger co-maker; the Manager writes the reason for the decision |

"Points to check" should name the action, not only the finding: fell behind on earlier loans: check the payment history first; penalties: ask the reason; restructured: confirm the new schedule was kept; carrying open loans: confirm their balances and the combined monthly due; applying again soon: ask why another loan is needed. Move the 8, 15 and 30 per 100 figures, the score date, the model version and "cached" into "Show technical detail".

**Thresholds: one rule everywhere.** The model decides: Low below 0.1036 (10.4 percent), Medium from 0.1036 to 0.2171, High from 0.2171 (21.7 percent), read from `GET /api/risk/model-info`. Where it differs today:
