## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the grounded prestige, mutual trust, and educational stewardship of a venerable rural teachers' cooperative. Tailored for grassroots educators and civic community members in Tubungan, Iloilo, the aesthetic pairs the institutional authority of an established financial cooperative with the genuine warmth of a Filipino communal organization (*bayanihan*).

The aesthetic movement combines **Corporate / Modern FinTech** with **Refined Editorial Warmth**. It eschews sterile institutional banking coldness in favor of dignified botanical depths, warm paper tones, and precise craftsmanship. Key characteristics include:
- Deep forest and vibrant growth greens reflecting financial health and local agricultural heritage.
- Warm ivory and parchment paper backgrounds instead of harsh clinical whites.
- Balanced structure communicating safety, transparent governance, and multi-generational reliability.

## Colors

The palette grounds members with trustworthy, deep botanical greens while energizing interactive call-to-actions with high-vitality emerald accents.

- **Primary (`#155234`)**: Deep Forest Green. Anchors primary banners, key header navigation, trust badges, and institutional iconography.
- **Secondary (`#16a34a`)**: Cooperative Emerald Green. Applied intentionally for active interactions, key buttons, balance yields, and growth progression indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#0b2319`)**: Forest Night. Used for deep-toned anchor footers, high-contrast modal surfaces, and legal reassurance sections.
- **Neutral (`#faf9f5`)**: Warm Ivory Canvas. Forms the standard page background, easing optical strain and conveying an inviting physical paper texture.
- **Named Surfaces & Borders**:
  - `surface-card` (`#ffffff`) combined with `border-sage` (`#e2e8e0`) delivers clear card definition without severe black borders.
  - `surface-cream-tint` (`#f2f6f1`) establishes rhythmic alternation between dashboard widgets and informational sections.

## Typography

The typography unites traditional academic credibility with modern digital legibility:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (`Source Serif 4`)**: Selected for its sturdy, humanist serifs that honor the academic heritage of teachers. It conveys institutional integrity, legal safety, and quiet respect across annual reports, hero calls, and dividend announcements.
- **Body & Interactive Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly, open letterforms that remain crisp across mobile micro-screens and financial ledgers. Used across forms, account balances, savings plans, navigation links, and administrative controls.
- Numeric metrics (loan amortizations, interest rates, share capital) must use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within `Plus Jakarta Sans` to maintain columnar alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by maximum widths for readability:
- **Max Content Width**: 1280px (`max-w-7xl`) for dashboards and main cooperative portals; 768px (`max-w-3xl`) for editorial member stories and legal policies.
- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (<640px, 4 columns), Tablet (640px–1024px, 8 columns), Desktop (>1024px, 12 columns).
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows an 8px base rhythm (`space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Section containers alternate between `neutral_color_hex` (`#faf9f5`) and `surface-cream-tint` (`#f2f6f1`) to create rhythmic visual pauses across content-heavy portals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp borders coupled with gentle, ambient warm green shadows:

- **Depth Philosophy**: Avoid heavy drop shadows or harsh synthetic glows. Surfaces float naturally using warm-tinted ambient diffusion.
- **Base Level (Resting Cards & Panels)**: Pure white background, bordered with 1px `border-sage` (`#e2e8e0`), accompanied by `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Level (Dropdowns & Interactive Cards on Hover)**: Retains the subtle border, lifted with `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Drawers**: Enclosed with 1px `border-sage` and reinforced by `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.06)`. Backdrops must utilize a deep forest tinted overlay (`rgba(11, 35, 25, 0.6)`) with 4px backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The interface balances welcoming accessibility with structured financial order:
- **Buttons, Inputs, Badges, and Chips**: Use standard rounding (8px / `0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Panels, and Modals**: Employ extended rounding (`rounded-xl` / 16px / `1rem`), generating an approachable, tactile container format.
- **Pill Accents**: Limited strictly to numerical status tags (e.g., "Active Loan", "Dividend Released") using `rounded-full`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Deposit, Apply for Loan, Join)**: Solid `secondary_color_hex` (`#16a34a`) background with white text, font weight 600, padding 10px 20px, `rounded-md`. Hover shifts to `#15803d` with a soft green glow.
- **Secondary / Institutional (View Ledger, Download Report)**: Solid `primary_color_hex` (`#155234`) with pure white text.
- **Outline / Neutral**: Pure white background, 1px `border-sage` border, and text set in `text-on-light` (`#0f241a`). Hover background shifts to `surface-cream-tint` (`#f2f6f1`).

### Cards & Content Containers
- Standard member cards, loan calculators, and announcement widgets use `surface-card` (`#ffffff`) background, 1px solid `border-sage` border, `rounded-xl`, and resting elevation.
- Internal card headers should feature a thin bottom divider (`1px solid #e2e8e0`) separating the category label and cooperative insignia from card content.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Input fields display an off-white resting state (`#fcfbf7`), 1px solid `border-sage` (`#e2e8e0`), text in `text-on-light`, and placeholder in `text-muted`.
- Focus state: Outline transitions to 1.5px solid `secondary_color_hex` (`#16a34a`) paired with a 3px ambient ring of `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Approval / Active**: Background `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.12)`, text `#155234`, border 1px solid `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.3)`.
- **Under Review / Processing**: Background `rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.12)`, text `#854d0e`, border 1px solid `rgba(234, 179, 8, 0.3)`.
- **General Metadata**: Background `surface-cream-tint`, text `text-muted`, border 1px solid `border-sage`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18px × 18px with `border-sage` when unchecked.
- Checked state fills with `primary_color_hex` (`#155234`) displaying a crisp white interior icon. Focus rings leverage `#16a34a` at 20% opacity.

### Specialized Cooperative Components
- **Member Ledger Card**: Split container with deep forest header band for share-capital and dividend totals, transitioning to clean white line-item transaction rows below.
- **Community Notice Board**: Warm ivory card bordered by an accent left-rail of `#16a34a` (4px width), prioritizing cooperative assembly updates and member welfare bulletins.
